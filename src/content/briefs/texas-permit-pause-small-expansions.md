**Finding, 21 September 2026:** the governor directed the Texas Commission on Environmental
Quality (TCEQ) to pause the issuance of all permits related to data center projects until
ERCOT completes its review. Whether that reaches an existing facility adding a few megawatts is
not yet known.

## What is known

- The letter sets **no size threshold**, does not define a data center project, and names no
  permit types.
- A TCEQ spokesperson told Houston Public Media the agency has paused air and water permits and
  authorizations for infrastructure directly supporting data centers. TCEQ has not published an
  implementation notice.
- TCEQ's update to the governor is due **19 October 2026**.

## What isn't known

- Whether the pause reaches authorizations for an existing facility's expansion of a few megawatts.
- Whether applications can still be filed and reviewed while issuance is paused.
- When the pause ends. The letter ties it to ERCOT completing its review, not to a date.

ERCOT's own actions, a 75 MW energization pause and a 25 MW information request, have their own
thresholds and are separate from the TCEQ pause.

## If you are planning a Texas addition, check

1. Which TCEQ authorizations the addition needs (for example, an air permit or registration for
   generator engines, or a stormwater authorization), and ask TCEQ in writing whether each is on hold.
2. Where your site's load sits against ERCOT's 75 MW and 25 MW thresholds.
3. TCEQ's 19 October update.
