**Finding, August 2026 (preliminary):** the producer price index for switchgear and switchboard
apparatus manufacturing was 13.3% higher than a year earlier.

## What the index shows

| BLS industry series, not seasonally adjusted | Aug 2025 | Aug 2026 (preliminary) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Switchgear and switchboard apparatus manufacturing (PCU335313335313) | 363.074 | 411.453 | **+13.3%** |
| Power, distribution and specialty transformer manufacturing (PCU335311335311) | 440.550 | 475.065 | +7.8% |
| Motor and generator manufacturing (PCU335312335312) | 306.239 | 318.130 | +3.9% |

## What this is, and what it isn't

- **A change in prices U.S. producers in that industry receive** for what they make. It is not
  a quote, an installed cost or a lead time.
- **Preliminary.** BLS revises these indexes for up to four months after first publication.
- **One series, named exactly.** BLS also publishes a broader commodity grouping, "switchgear,
  switchboard, and industrial controls equipment", which rose 12.3% over the same year. Both
  are correct; they measure different things, so they should not be mixed or averaged.

## If you have electrical equipment specified but not yet bought

Check whether your budget and any escalation clause assume a price rise of this size, and when
the quote you are relying on expires.
