## Estimates, ranges, and information that goes stale

This matters more than anything else on this page.

- **Most figures DCU publishes are other organizations' numbers, not DCU's.** When a research firm,
  practitioner, vendor, or government body reports a lead time or benchmark, DCU reports what that
  source said, attributed to it. DCU does not adopt it as its own forecast.
- **Lead times and market conditions change.** A figure that was accurate when published may be
  wrong weeks later. The date attached to a figure is part of the figure.
- **Ranges and approximations are exactly that.** Where a value is read from a chart that prints no
  numbers, or is otherwise approximate, DCU marks it as approximate. An approximation is not a quote
  and not a commitment from anyone.
- **A market benchmark may not describe your project.** Equipment class, capacity, voltage,
  configuration, region, and supplier all change the answer. DCU preserves those distinctions where
  the source provides them and says so where it does not.
- **Regulatory and permitting information is time-sensitive.** Comment periods close, rules take
  effect, and agencies revise what they have published. Always check the underlying record before
  relying on a date or requirement.
