**Status:** Proposed · substituted and recommended for passage by the Zoning, Neighborhoods & Development Committee, 6 October 2026 · **Next:** Common Council vote · **Checked:** 8 October 2026

> **For an existing enterprise facility.** The pause covers *enlarging* an existing data center,
> not only new ones. A server room inside an office, hospital or other building with a different
> principal use is excluded. A standalone enterprise data center planning an addition in Milwaukee
> would need to know whether the ordinance passes, and when.

## What it would do

The substitute ordinance prohibits "the establishment of new data centers or enlargement of existing
data centers" while it is in effect. No city department, board or officer could approve an application,
permit, occupancy, zoning or site-plan request "to establish, construct, enlarge, expand, convert, or
occupy a data center." The prohibition applies however the facility is described in an application,
if its principal use meets the definition. It does not apply to planned development districts.

It would last for the lesser of one year after passage, or until the zoning code is amended to regulate
data centers. A separate file (260142) proposes that zoning framework.

## Who it affects

A "data center" is a facility used primarily to operate, maintain or provide access to computer and
network facilities, together with accessory uses such as offices, air handlers, power generators,
generator fuel storage, water cooling and utility infrastructure. It **does not** mean "a computer
server room or a facility that serves another principal use on the same premises." The ordinance sets
no size threshold.

## If you operate or plan a data center in Milwaukee, check

1. Whether your facility's principal use is the data center, or whether it is accessory to another use.
2. Whether any permit or approval for an addition, conversion or new equipment yard is pending, and
   when the Council vote is scheduled.
3. Whether file 260142, the zoning framework, sets standards that would apply after the pause.
