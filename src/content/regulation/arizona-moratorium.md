**Status:** Enacted, effective 12 September 2026 · **Applies to:** applications from 1 July 2026 through 30 June 2029

## What changed

Arizona has suspended new applications to its Computer Data Center program.
HB 4168, Chapter 140 of the 2026 session, bars the Arizona Commerce Authority
from accepting applications for a new computer data center during the
moratorium window, and provides that no new computer data center can qualify
for the tax relief under A.R.S. § 41-1519 during that period.

One timing note. Arizona's general effective date for 2026 session legislation
is 12 September 2026, but the moratorium provision applies retroactively to
1 July 2026.

## Who it applies to

Any entity that would have sought a new Computer Data Center certification in
Arizona between 1 July 2026 and 30 June 2029.

The program's qualifying thresholds are $50 million of investment in Maricopa or
Pima County, and $25 million in counties with populations of 800,000 or less.

We have not established how many Arizona certifications historically went to
enterprise-owned facilities rather than colocation providers or hyperscale
operators.

## What the law actually does

It closes the application window. It does not change the tax treatment available
under an existing certification, and it does not repeal § 41-1519.

The distinction the statute draws is between a *new* computer data center and
facilities already in the program. Section 41-1519 provides that existing
certifications remain in effect absent specified revocation, and expressly
permits expansion of certified sustainable redevelopment projects on the same or
contiguous property.

What remains unclear is when an expansion or additional phase can remain within
an existing certification, and when it would require a separate new computer
data center certification — which the moratorium would block.

## Why an enterprise data center owner should care

If Arizona was on a site-selection list partly because of the Computer Data
Center incentive, the incentive is unavailable to a new facility for the next
three years.

A project weighing Arizona against other markets on total cost now has one fewer
variable working in Arizona's favor.

## What we can and cannot conclude yet

We have verified the statute, its chapter number, the moratorium window, the
retroactive application date, and the program's investment thresholds.

Under current law, the moratorium ends after 30 June 2029, while § 41-1519
continues to authorize new certifications through 31 December 2033. Future
legislation could change that.

The open question is the boundary described above — when an expansion stays
inside an existing certification and when it needs a new one. We have also not
reviewed the legislative record for what prompted the moratorium.
