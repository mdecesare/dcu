## What this quarter's data shows

**Equipment class can produce more variation within one source than is visible
between broad category benchmarks.** Terrapin's transformer bands run from 20
weeks to more than 150, all from one source in one quarter under one
methodology. Their MV switchgear bands span more than seventy weeks.

**A category benchmark may or may not fall inside the relevant band.** JLL's UPS
figure lands inside Terrapin's 500–1,500 kVA range. Their generator figure sits
above Terrapin's 500–1,000 kW range. Nothing in the category figure indicates
which case applies.

**Summary ranges are not interchangeable with detailed bands.** Terrapin
publishes both, and the summary compresses several detailed bands into one
range. A figure quoted from a summary table may not match the banded figure for
the same equipment.

**Coverage is uneven.** Transformers, generators and MV switchgear have
class-banded figures from a practitioner source. Chillers have practitioner
ranges but no tonnage or equipment-class bands. LV switchgear, ATS and PDU have
no banded figures at all in the current dataset, which makes those numbers
harder to apply to a specific project.

## Methodology and limits

These figures are compiled from published sources. DCU does not quote equipment,
does not have transactional visibility of its own, and does not present these
ranges as prices or as commitments from any manufacturer.

Lead times vary by manufacturer, configuration, quantity, voltage class and
order timing. Terrapin states their figures derive from active 2026 manufacturer
slot reservations and TCG project procurement data. JLL's figures here are
read from an unlabelled chart in its January 2026 report (see the note at the
top of this page). SourceBlue operates in procurement and supply chain
execution. SAVRN sells infrastructure solutions.

Where a figure could not be traced to its original publisher, it is omitted from
this page. Full approach: [Methodology](/methodology).
