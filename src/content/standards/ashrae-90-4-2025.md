ASHRAE describes 90.4 as code-intended, like Standard 90.1, which it references for the building envelope,
service water heating and lighting. ASHRAE lists the 2025 edition's changes as an expanded purpose that
allows for considerations beyond direct energy use, updated definitions of process heat and ventilation,
and refinements to the UPS and electrical loss calculations, including that UPS efficiency is based on the
total available UPS capacity, redundant capacity included.

No public review of 90.4 was open on ASHRAE's public review page when we checked.
