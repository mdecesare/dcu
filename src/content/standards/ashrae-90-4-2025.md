A 2025 edition of ASHRAE Standard 90.4, the energy standard written for data centers, is current. It
is intended for adoption into energy codes, and it relies on Standard 90.1 for the building envelope,
service water heating and lighting. Which edition applies to your project depends on the energy code your
jurisdiction has adopted, so the same expansion can face different requirements in different places.

No public review of 90.4 was open on ASHRAE's public review page when we checked.
